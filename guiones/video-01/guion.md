Voiceover:

Every year, thousands of Americans pack their bags to study, teach, and do research abroad,  fully funded by a program built to bring nations closer together. And if your destination is Mexico, this is where your journey begins.

The Fulbright Program is the flagship international exchange program of the U.S. government. It was created in 1946 by Senator J. William Fulbright with a simple but ambitious goal: to build peace and mutual understanding between people through education. Today, Fulbright operates in more than 160 countries. Its alumni include Nobel laureates, Pulitzer Prize winners, and heads of state — proof of what can happen when talented people are given the chance to share ideas.

* 62 Nobel laureates  
* 93 Pulitzer Prize winners  
* 44 heads of state

In Mexico, the Fulbright Program is run by COMEXUS — the Mexico–United States Commission for Educational and Cultural Exchange. Founded in 1990 and funded by both governments, COMEXUS administers the Fulbright–García Robles grants. The name honors two people: Senator Fulbright, and Alfonso García Robles, the Mexican diplomat who won the Nobel Peace Prize in 1982 for his work on nuclear disarmament. For 35 years, COMEXUS has been the bridge that makes this exchange possible — in both directions, between the United States and Mexico.

* November 27, 1990 → 35 years

If you're an American student, recent graduate, teacher, or researcher, COMEXUS opens a path to live and work in Mexico — fully funded. Through Fulbright–García Robles, you can pursue graduate research, teach English in Mexican classrooms as an English Teaching Assistant, or bring your expertise to a university. Across nine programs, whatever your field, there's likely one built for you.

For more than 35 years, COMEXUS has supported over 5,680 Mexican and American grantees — a growing community of educators, scientists, artists, and leaders who came back changed, and who keep the U.S.–Mexico relationship strong from the ground up. When you join Fulbright–García Robles, you join them.
